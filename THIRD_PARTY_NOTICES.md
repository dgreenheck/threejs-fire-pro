## Demo meshes and PBR materials — Poly Haven (CC0)

The scene assets under `editor/scenes/assets/` include locally bundled 1K
materials and meshes from Poly Haven. They are distributed under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Poly Haven asset license: https://polyhaven.com/license

- Rock 07 — Jenelle van Heerden: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07
  Original topology: 14,844 triangles; base color, OpenGL normal, packed AO/roughness/metalness.
- Dead Tree Trunk 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/dead_tree_trunk_02
  Original topology: 83,128 triangles; base color, OpenGL normal, packed AO/roughness/metalness.
- Red Brick 03: https://polyhaven.com/a/red_brick_03
- Concrete Wall 006: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_006
- Metal Plate 02 — Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02

Texture sets use separate base color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. Color
maps are sRGB; physical-data maps are linear. Model images are embedded in GLB
without reauthoring the artist's UVs. Meshes and textures are shared among scene
instances. No generated images are used. Source dimensions are normalized into
meter-scale demo props; scene Y is up. The nozzle is modeled along local +X.
